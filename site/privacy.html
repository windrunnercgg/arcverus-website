Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This page explains what information the ArcVerus website collects, why, and how you can opt out. If you have a question, email us at info@arcverus.com.

Analytics tools

We use these tools today:

  • Google Analytics 4, to understand how people use the site.
  • Google Tag Manager, which loads our analytics tools.

We may add these tools in the future:

  • Google Ads, to measure whether our ads lead to contact requests (conversion tracking) and to show our ads to people who have visited the site (remarketing).
  • Meta Pixel, for the same purposes on Facebook and Instagram.
  • Microsoft Clarity, to see how people move through our pages.

We’ll update this page if we start using any of them.

What these tools collect

  • The pages you view, including which service pages you look at
  • What you click on
  • Your device and browser type
  • Your approximate location (city or region, based on your internet connection)

These tools use cookies: small files stored by your browser that let them recognize repeat visits. The companies that provide the tools (Google, and Meta or Microsoft if we add them) receive this information and handle it under their own privacy policies.

The contact form

When you send a message through our contact form, your name, email address, and message are emailed to info@arcverus.com through our email delivery provider, Resend. We use them only to reply to you.

The form also records which of our service pages you came from, so we know what you’re asking about. To remember this while you browse, the site uses your browser’s temporary session storage, which is cleared when you close the tab.

Server logs

Like most websites, our web server keeps standard logs of requests, including your IP address and browser type. We use them to keep the site running and secure, and to stop spam sent through the contact form.

How to opt out

  • Google Analytics: install Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Ads: manage ad personalization in Google’s ad settings. If we add Meta or Microsoft tools, you can also use Meta’s ad preferences and Microsoft’s ad settings.
  • Cookies: your browser’s settings let you block or delete cookies, for all sites or just this one. The site still works without them.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email info@arcverus.com.
